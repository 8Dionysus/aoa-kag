# AOA-KAG-D-0042 Semantic Owner Evidence For Budget Admission

## Index Metadata

- Decision ID: AOA-KAG-D-0042
- Original date: 2026-08-21
- Surface classes: schema contract, generated-delta validator, owner procedure
- KAG surfaces: portable family, budget receipt, semantic admission evidence
- Source lanes: aoa-kag, downstream owner-family consumers
- Guard families: source-owned authority, exact identity, fail-closed admission
- Posture: accepted

## Context

The portable-family budget receipt correctly binds a measured generated delta,
tracked size, base ref, and head family digest, but its owner reason is a free
form string. A structurally valid receipt can therefore authorize a large
generated change without proving that authored source or an owner-owned
topology decision caused the change. The resulting receipt is an admission
envelope, not semantic evidence.

The pressure is generic: a generated family may grow through legitimate source
migration, schema or builder work, topology pressure, or accidental repeated
materialization. The owner needs to distinguish those states without using a
repository name, incident wording, version, hash, or one-off size exception.

## Decision

Keep the budget receipt as a mechanical, digest-bound measurement envelope and
add a separate typed semantic-evidence packet generated by the `aoa-kag`
owner procedure. The packet binds the exact owner, base and head family
identities, source snapshot, procedure/schema digests, authored-source delta,
generated-path measurement, duplicate-materialization signal, typed cause, and
an authored decision reference. The receipt carries the evidence path and
digest and is accepted for an exceedance only when the packet recomputes to
`supported`.

Legacy v1 receipts remain structural-only and resolve to `migration_required`;
they are never grandfathered into semantic approval. Evidence states preserve
`supported`, `unsupported`, `unknown`, and `migration_required`. Source,
parity, fixed-point, standing-budget, global-ceiling, compatibility, and
hosted owner gates remain independent and blocking. This contract does not
claim consumer admission, runtime health, proof, publication, or human
acceptance.

## Options Considered

- Reject every generated family over the default delta: rejected because it
  would punish legitimate source-owned migrations and topology changes.
- Treat a longer reason, a digest, or a changed schema version as semantic
  approval: rejected because prose and identity alone do not explain causality.
- Add a one-off rejection for the observed large receipt: rejected because it
  would encode incident history instead of repairing the owner invariant.
- Add typed, identity-bound owner evidence while retaining all existing gates:
  chosen because it separates measurement from semantic cause and preserves
  compatibility through an explicit migration state.

## Rationale

`aoa-kag` owns the derived family structure, receipt schema, and validator
boundary, while authored meaning remains in owner source surfaces. Measuring
the authored-source delta and the generated-path delta gives the validator a
reproducible causal signal without pretending that an amplification ratio is
itself a quality judgment. Exact base/head/procedure/review bindings prevent
copying evidence across candidates, stale source procedures, or unrelated
families. Duplicate materialization and accidental amplification remain
unsupported rather than being normalized into approval.

## Consequences

- New exceedance receipts require a v2 receipt and a matching v1 evidence
  packet produced by the owner procedure.
- A missing, copied, stale, tampered, unsupported, unknown, or legacy packet
  blocks semantic admission and preserves the reason for the block.
- Large legitimate source-owned changes can still be admitted when the typed
  evidence is supported; the standing and aggregate ceilings cannot be raised
  by a receipt.
- Downstream consumers must migrate to the typed contract; this decision does
  not mutate their repositories or declare their family, runtime, proof, or
  landing state accepted.

## Validation

The source-owned unit tests cover supported, unsupported, unknown, and legacy
migration states plus wrong identity, tamper, stale, copied, pathological
duplication, and standing-budget cases. Run the paired schema/example check,
the repository-declared source-fast and generated lanes, the decision-index
and decision-record validators, and the owner-family/release checks. Downstream
canaries remain read-only evidence; their receipts and landing state are not
changed by this decision.
